- REF-03 is retained because it is useful for low-cost adsorbent selection, capacity, kinetics and techno-economic considerations. Its subject matter is broad wastewater treatment rather than specifically antibiotic adsorption.
- REF-04 addresses pharmaceutical compounds and specifically reviews antibiotic classes and carbon-based adsorption; it is useful for carbon adsorbents and adsorption chemistry.
- REF-05 is a dedicated review of antibiotic adsorption by natural clay minerals, including structural and solution-chemistry factors.
- REF-01 is tetracycline-centred and provides a comparison of treatment technologies; do not generalise all tetracycline-specific details to every antibiotic.
- REF-02 is the broadest antibiotic-focused biomass/biochar review in this batch and explicitly covers isotherms, kinetics, regeneration and engineering prospects.
