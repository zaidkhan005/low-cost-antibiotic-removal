For each file, record:
- PDF filename and mapped REF-ID;
- file opens and page count;
- first-page metadata agrees with inventory;
- DOI and journal details;
- full text completeness;
- figures, tables, equations and supplementary information availability;
- text extraction quality and pages requiring visual inspection;
- topics relevant to the 15-slide outline;
