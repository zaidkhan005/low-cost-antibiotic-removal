# Low-Cost Antibiotic Removal from Aqueous Medium

Academic review presentation project: **Low-Cost Adsorbents for the Removal of Antibiotics from Aqueous Medium**.

This repository is separate from the manuscript project `reviewAdsorbants`. It supports a fundamentals-first, review-paper-style academic presentation that explains the subject progressively and evaluates adsorption science, experimental evidence, regeneration, and practical feasibility.

## Project constraints

- **Reference collection:** fixed at 15 selected papers. No additional papers are to be added unless the project owner explicitly changes this decision.
- **Presentation target:** approximately 15 slides.
- **Presentation style:** academic review presentation with textbook-like progression, not a summary of the user's earlier manuscript.
- **PowerPoint production:** do not begin until literature extraction, evidence organisation, and slide-by-slide content have been reviewed.

## Workflow

1. Audit the 15 downloaded PDFs and verify their bibliographic details and full-text accessibility.
2. Extract definitions, concepts, equations, models, mechanisms, experimental conditions, results, limitations, regeneration evidence, and cost information.
3. Build a traceable evidence matrix linking each claim to its source and page/table/figure where possible.
4. Draft and review content for the approved 15-slide structure.
5. Create and quality-check the editable PowerPoint.

## Repository structure

- `references/` — fixed reference inventory and PDF audit.
- `literature-extraction/` — topic-based scientific extraction.
- `evidence-matrix/` — source-to-claim evidence tracking.
- `presentation-content/` — slide-by-slide draft and citations.
- `diagrams/` — original/adapted diagrams and source notes.
- `powerpoint/` — final editable presentation and exports.

## Scientific quality rules

- Distinguish adsorption from absorption and removal from degradation.
- Distinguish observed experimental uptake from model-fitted capacities and derived values.
- Do not infer a molecular mechanism solely from a kinetic or isotherm model fit.
- Report experimental conditions with quantitative results.
- Treat regeneration, adsorbent recovery, secondary waste, and process costs as part of practical feasibility.
- Cite every scientific claim and identify the relevant source precisely.
- Do not fabricate missing experimental details or costs.
