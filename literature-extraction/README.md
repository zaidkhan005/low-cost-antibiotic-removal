# Literature Extraction

Topic notes will be written only after the corresponding PDFs have been audited.

Planned notes:
- `fundamentals.md`: antibiotic classes, aqueous media, pollution and treatment methods.
- `adsorption-mechanisms.md`: adsorption terminology, adsorbent types, surface interactions and influencing factors.
- `isotherms.md`: equilibrium models and assumptions.
- `kinetics.md`: kinetic models, rate interpretation and limitations.
- `thermodynamics.md`: thermodynamic parameters and interpretation.
- `regeneration.md`: desorption, regeneration methods, reuse cycles and residual waste.
- `cost-analysis.md`: material production, recovery, regeneration, scale-up and cost metrics.

Every substantial statement should carry a REF-ID and page/table/figure locator.

## Completed extraction records

- [Stage 2 Extraction 01 — Fundamentals and Adsorption Mechanisms](fundamentals-and-adsorption-mechanisms.md): source-grounded notes for antibiotic/water context, terminology, material families, adsorption sequence, mechanisms and controlling factors. Explicitly labels teaching definitions that are not directly defined in the 15-paper corpus.
- [Stage 2 Extraction 02 — Quantitative Adsorption Evaluation](quantitative-adsorption-evaluation.md): batch workflow, equilibrium/time-dependent mass balance, removal efficiency, a derived REF-09 worked example, interpretation of capacity versus removal, and the REF-15 equation-sign anomaly.
- [Stage 2 Extraction 03 — Adsorption Isotherms](adsorption-isotherms.md): Langmuir, Freundlich, Temkin and Sips equations/interpretations; comparison of REF-09, REF-10 and REF-14; nonlinear regression/error criteria; and source-level parameter/equation cautions.
- [Stage 2 Extraction 04 — Adsorption Kinetics](adsorption-kinetics.md): PFO, PSO, Weber–Morris and Elovich equations; REF-07's mechanism and regression cautions; and antibiotic-specific kinetic comparisons from REF-09, REF-10 and REF-14, including the missing REF-10 ESI parameter-table limitation.
- [Stage 2 Extraction 05 — Thermodynamics of Adsorption](adsorption-thermodynamics.md): Gibbs, enthalpy, entropy and Van't Hoff relationships; temperature-dependent antibiotic-uptake evidence from REF-09, REF-13, REF-14 and REF-15; and explicit standard-state, missing-supplementary-data and fitted-versus-observed-value cautions.
- [Stage 2 Extraction 06 — Regeneration and Reusability](regeneration-and-reusability.md): desorption versus regeneration versus capacity retention; cycle evidence from REF-12, REF-13 and REF-15; release results and limitations from REF-11 and REF-14; and the regenerant, secondary-waste and cost considerations in REF-02, REF-03 and REF-08.
- [Stage 2 Extraction 07 — Economics and Practical Feasibility](economics-and-practical-feasibility.md): whole-process cost components; preparation/activation steps reported by REF-12–REF-15; general and pharmaceutical-wide cost examples from REF-03 and REF-08; real-water, recovery, regeneration and secondary-waste considerations; and explicit missing-cost and source-scope cautions.
