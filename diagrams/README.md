# Diagrams

Source figures for the review presentation are organised by paper under `source-figures/<paper-slug>/`. The [Source Figure Register](source-figures/INDEX.md) records figure captions, intended slide placement, DOIs, attribution and scientific-use notes for Ajala et al. (2023), Teo et al. (2022), Perelomov et al. (2025), and Chen et al. (2024).

The register includes:
- Ajala: environmental sources/pathways and biomass sorption mechanism.
- Teo: antibiotic class structures, carbon-based adsorbents and carbon–antibiotic interactions.
- Perelomov: publication trend, clay structural units, clay mineral crystallography and antibiotic–clay mechanisms.
- Chen: magnetic biochar characterisation, XPS before/after adsorption, pH effects, kinetics, isotherms, proposed OTC mechanism, regeneration and Fe leaching.

Preserve source attribution on slides and in the bibliography. Experimental figures must retain readable axes, units, conditions and legends. Conceptual mechanism diagrams must not be presented as universal proof of every proposed interaction.

**Binary assets:** Download the companion source-figure bundle and extract it at the repository root to add the actual PNGs. The index/register being committed does not by itself mean the image binaries are already present in this Git tree.

- Liang et al. (2024): seven figures covering NFO/BC morphology, phase/porosity/FT-IR/magnetic characterisation, XPS, TC solution effects and reuse, TC isotherms and kinetics, and before/after adsorption characterisation.
