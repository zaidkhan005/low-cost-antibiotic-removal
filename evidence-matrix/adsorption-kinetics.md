## Evidence rules for slide 11

- Plot and discuss \(q_t\) versus time in the original coordinates.
- State time units and fitted rate-constant units consistently.
- Distinguish fitted \(q_e\) from measured or mass-balance-derived values.
- Show that PFO and PSO are empirical kinetic descriptions, not one-to-one mechanism labels.
- Use Weber–Morris segments/intercepts as evidence about possible transport stages, with qualifications.
- Present REF-09, REF-10 and REF-14 as separate case studies, not a controlled head-to-head performance ranking.
- Report the fitting method and its goodness-of-fit criterion; for nonlinear model comparison, include AIC/BIC where available.
- Mark REF-10's missing ESI parameter table as an evidence limitation.
