## Evidence rules for slide 9

- State concentration, volume and mass units explicitly.
- Keep \(q_e\), \(q_t\), fitted \(q_m\), and percentage removal distinct.
- Mark all constructed/back-calculated values as derived, not measured.
- Do not compare results without the associated antibiotic, adsorbent and experimental conditions.
- Do not infer degradation from disappearance from the liquid phase.
- Use the volume-aware expression when sampling changes liquid volume.
- Preserve the REF-15 sign anomaly as a source note; use REF-09 and REF-14 for the positive mass-balance formulas.
