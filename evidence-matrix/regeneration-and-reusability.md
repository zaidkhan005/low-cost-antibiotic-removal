## Evidence rules for slide 13

- Distinguish desorption, regeneration performance and reusability/capacity retention.
- Do not describe a release experiment as proof of multi-cycle regeneration.
- Preserve the exact metric used in each primary paper: capacity retention versus removal percentage.
- Show adsorbent separation and regeneration as separate process steps.
- Include regenerant volume, concentration, energy/time and rinsing/drying needs when the source provides them.
- Treat secondary regenerant/wash streams and material leaching as part of the process, not as invisible laboratory details.
- Label REF-03 and REF-08 as general/pharmaceutical-wide context rather than antibiotic-specific quantitative evidence.
- Do not directly rank cycle results across REF-12, REF-13 and REF-15 because the materials, antibiotics, regeneration media, conditions and reported metrics differ.
