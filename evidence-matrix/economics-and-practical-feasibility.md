## Evidence rules for slide 14

- Do not equate waste-derived with zero-cost or low-cost.
- Label REF-03 as general wastewater context and REF-08 as pharmaceutical-wide review context.
- Do not present REF-08's IBU/BPA costs as CIP/TC/OTC costs.
- Preserve the REF-08 Table 5 versus conclusion cost-range discrepancy.
- Describe REF-15's cost-saving conclusion as qualitative because a full cost calculation is absent.
- Report preparation and regeneration steps that reveal likely cost drivers, without assigning invented prices.
- Keep €/kg adsorbent, €/L or €/m³ treated water, and cost per mass of antibiotic removed distinct.
- Treat real-water performance, adsorbent recovery, regenerant treatment and spent-solid disposal as essential practical variables.
