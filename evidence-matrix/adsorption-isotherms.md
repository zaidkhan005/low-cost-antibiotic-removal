## Evidence rules for slide 10

- Show \(C_e\) and \(q_e\) with their units and clarify that the curve is an equilibrium relationship.
- Use the REF-14 Sips expression on the slide; preserve the REF-06 printed-form discrepancy in the research notes.
- Distinguish fitted \(q_m\), \(q_{mS}\), \(q_{mT}\), and \(q_s\) from observed or mass-balance-derived uptake.
- Compare candidate models within the same experimental dataset, not across unrelated studies.
- Report the fit criterion used (\(R^2\), ARE or another error function).
- Do not infer a unique adsorption mechanism from isotherm fit alone.
- Attach antibiotic, adsorbent, pH, temperature, concentration range and equilibrium conditions to reported comparisons.
