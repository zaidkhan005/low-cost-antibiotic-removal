## Evidence rules

- Keep the source's exact scope: a general wastewater review is not antibiotic-specific evidence.
- Every quantitative result requires units, experimental conditions, and whether it is observed, derived or fitted.
- A model fit alone does not establish a molecular mechanism.
- Never imply adsorption is degradation without independent evidence of transformation.
- Recover precise page/table/figure locators before slide-level citation when they were absent from the branch-chat transfer.
