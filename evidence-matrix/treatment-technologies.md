## Slide-level guardrails

1. The comparison is about process role and documented limitations, not a universal effectiveness or cost ranking.
2. Keep the biological-treatment statement general but evidence-qualified; keep the complete-removal example explicitly tetracycline-specific.
3. Keep AOP transformation separate from mineralisation and toxicity reduction.
4. Identify membrane fouling, energy demand and cost as source-reported constraints; distinguish nanofiltration/ultrafiltration from reverse osmosis.
5. Describe adsorption as liquid-to-solid transfer and retain the spent-adsorbent/recovered-antibiotic management issue.
6. Presentation-facing citations use author–date format. REF-01, REF-04 and REF-15 are internal evidence-tracking identifiers only.
