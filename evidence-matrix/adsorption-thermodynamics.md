## Evidence rules for slide 12

- Use the equations as reported in REF-13, preserve its printed \(\Delta G\), \(\Delta H\), and \(\Delta S\) symbols without added degree superscripts, and state the source's \(K_d\) notation.
- Explain the slope and intercept from the Van't Hoff relation; identify them as equation-derived.
- Use REF-13 as the main numerical thermodynamics example, but do not reconstruct missing Table S4 values.
- Use REF-14's temperature-dependent uptake as a clear measured/derived response example and report its conditions.
- Distinguish REF-15's Langmuir-fitted \(q_m\) values from thermodynamic parameters.
- Treat REF-09's endothermic conclusion as qualitative because its Supporting Information is missing.
- Do not present REF-12 as a thermodynamic result source until its detailed values are available in the supplied source set.
- Keep D–R energy \(E\) distinct from \(\Delta H\).
- Include the equilibrium-constant/standard-state caveat in speaker notes.
