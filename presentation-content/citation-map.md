## Cross-slide audit flags

1. **Transferred methodological and study records:** Ayawei et al. (2017), Vareda (2023), Gkika et al. (2025), Mirizadeh et al. (2023), and Egbedina et al. (2021). The repository's Batch 2 handoff notes that some exact locators were not carried over. The locators used for slides 10 and 11 were extracted in the topic-specific pass, but any inherited quantitative statement not listed in those passes must not be added without checking the original PDF.
2. **Gkika et al. (2025) scope:** Pharmaceutical-wide review; not all numerical examples are antibiotic-specific.
3. **Mirizadeh et al. (2023) supporting information:** Not supplied; detailed thermodynamic values are therefore qualitative only.
4. **Egbedina et al. (2021) supporting information:** Not supplied; missing full kinetic/isotherm parameter tables must not be invented.
5. **Liang et al. (2024) thermodynamic Table S4:** Not supplied; do not reconstruct missing numerical ΔG or ΔS values.
6. **Meseguer et al. (2024) regeneration:** No completed regeneration-cycle data in the supplied main PDF; further tests are identified as future work.
7. **Zhao et al. (2024) capacity equation:** The printed sign convention is internally inconsistent with positive uptake; use the consistent positive mass-balance form supported by Mirizadeh et al. (2023)/Meseguer et al. (2024).
8. **Cost evidence:** Do not imply that a waste precursor has zero acquisition, preparation, recovery or disposal cost.
