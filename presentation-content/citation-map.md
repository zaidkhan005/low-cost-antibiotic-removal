## Cross-slide audit flags

1. **REF-06–REF-10 transferred records:** The repository's Batch 2 handoff notes that some exact locators were not carried over. The locators used for slides 10 and 11 were extracted in the topic-specific pass, but any inherited quantitative statement not listed in those passes must not be added without checking the original PDF.
2. **REF-08 scope:** Pharmaceutical-wide review; not all numerical examples are antibiotic-specific.
3. **REF-09 supporting information:** Not supplied; detailed thermodynamic values are therefore qualitative only.
4. **REF-10 supporting information:** Not supplied; missing full kinetic/isotherm parameter tables must not be invented.
5. **REF-13 thermodynamic Table S4:** Not supplied; do not reconstruct missing numerical ΔG or ΔS values.
6. **REF-14 regeneration:** No completed regeneration-cycle data in the supplied main PDF; further tests are identified as future work.
7. **REF-15 capacity equation:** The printed sign convention is internally inconsistent with positive uptake; use the consistent positive mass-balance form supported by REF-09/REF-14.
8. **Cost evidence:** Do not imply that a waste precursor has zero acquisition, preparation, recovery or disposal cost.
